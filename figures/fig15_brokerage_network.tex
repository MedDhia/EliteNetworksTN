  \caption{\textbf{The brokerage core of the Tunisian elite network.}
  Node area is proportional to betweenness centrality. The 191
  entities shown, joined by 397 ties, are the giant
  component of the subgraph induced on the 200 highest
  betweenness entities, which together hold 39.3\% of
  all betweenness in the graph. Selection is therefore on
  the quantity the node areas encode; the full network of 23,274 entities
  and 31,457 ties cannot be rendered legibly at page width. Ties are
  undirected and undated, pooling shareholding, board and governing-body
  membership, kinship, party and parliamentary structures, and the seed
  roster, over 1957--2026.
  The composition is 73 natural persons, 23 state bodies and
  parties, and 95 firms, associations and unions. The 12
  highest-brokerage entities are labelled. Abbreviations: État Tunisien, Etat Tunisien; Présidence, Présidence de la République; RCD, central committee of the RCD; BIAT, Banque Internationale Arabe de Tunisie; Min. Économie, Ministère de l'Économie Nationale; M. Trabelsi, Mohamed Trabelsi; Nidaa Tounes, Mouvement Nidaa Tounes; STB, Société Tunisienne de Banque; BNA, Banque Nationale Agricole.}
  \label{fig:fig15_brokerage_network}
