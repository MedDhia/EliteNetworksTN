Comparative Analysis of Institutional Gender Bias Across Five Political Regimes (1957–2026)

Comparing Bourguiba (1957–87), Ben Ali (1987–2011), Transition / Troika (2011–14), Essebsi (2014–19), and Kais Saied (2019–26) across 100,582 spells.

Data Source: Official Gazette of the Republic of Tunisia (JORT 1957–2026). Extracted from MedDhia/EliteNetworksTN.
Methodological Notes: Promotion wait-time delays are estimated using both two-sample Welch's t-tests and within-ministry fixed effects regressions controlling for baseline rank. Hierarchical funnel charts track representation across civil service rank tiers.
